// Data & Sources — read this

VERIFIED Attachment system. BF6 uses a point budget ("Pick-100"): 100 points per primary, 60 per secondary, spent across named slots (Optic, Muzzle, Barrel, Underbarrel, Magazine, Ammo, Stock). Confirmed on EA's official loadout page & Shacknews.
VERIFIED REDSEC health baseline. TTK is now computed against 180 effective HP — 100 base health + 80 armor (2 plates × 40 HP), per EA's official REDSEC armor update. Toggle to bare 100 HP in the Builder. (Armor also reduces weapon-vs-armor damage at close range, so real REDSEC TTK is a touch longer still — those per-weapon armor curves aren't published.)
VERIFIED Meta picks & the loadouts in the four class cards. Cross-referenced against battlefieldmeta.gg (Season 3, updated May 25 2026), Gamerant, Dexerto & wzstats.gg.
VERIFIED Per-attachment point costs. Now real in-game values — cross-referenced from the azerpug Battlefield Companion database (per-attachment pages) plus Steam screenshots for magazines. E.g. Standard Suppressor 20 · CQB Suppressor 30 · most optics 10 · Iron Sights 5 · Bipod 10 · Slim Angled 25 · Classic Vertical 35 · drum 45 · belt 55.
ESTIMATE Why one estimate is unavoidable: the Builder's stat deltas (+RCL, −ADS, etc.). EA publishes no granular per-attachment stat numbers, and azerpug only tracks a coarse Precision/Control/Mobility model — not "−12% recoil." So these few effect numbers can't be sourced exactly and stay as reasoned approximations. Stock-slot costs are likewise estimated (EA exposes no separate Stock slot). Everything else here is real.
APPROX Armory damage / RPM / mag figures. Pulled from community trackers (battlefieldredsec.space, skycoach). Some differ from in-game values — treat as directional. Verified figures where known: SGX 830 RPM / 217ms TTK, M2010 ESR ~43 RPM / 1-shot / 900 m/s.
