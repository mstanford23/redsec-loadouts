KORD 6P67
Assault Rifle

The most forgiving meta AR. ~900 RPM with near-zero recoil makes it the safest pick in any 1v1. Tap or burst to extend its effective range.

  • Near-zero recoil — beam at mid-range

  • Versatile across CQB and mid engagements
